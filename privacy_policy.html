Privacy Policy for Moon King

Effective Date: November 16, 2026

BoxOfBoxes ("I", "me", or "my") developed Moon King as a commercial, one-time purchase game. This page explains how the game handles user information.

1. No Data Collection

Moon King does not collect, store, or transmit any personal or device data.

  • No user accounts or sign-ins are required.
  • No analytics, tracking SDKs, or third-party advertisements are included in the game.
  • All game progress and settings are saved locally on your device only.

2. Google Play Services

Because you download and purchase Moon King through the Google Play Store, Google processes payment and basic app distribution data independently under its own policies. You can review the Google Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles store transactions.

3. Children’s Privacy

Because Moon King does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of consent in your country).

4. Changes to This Policy

If future updates to Moon King introduce features that require data collection, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly before those changes take effect.
